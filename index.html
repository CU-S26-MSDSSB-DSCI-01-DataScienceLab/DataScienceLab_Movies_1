Insights
6 Key Findings

What the data revealed about movie success across all 12 research questions.

🌍
Industry Shapes Ratings
Smaller, selective industries show higher averages because fewer, more curated releases appear in the dataset. Larger industries display greater variation.
🎭
Genre: Ratings ≠ Votes
Documentary and biopic genres earn high ratings with smaller audiences. Action and franchise genres drive massive vote counts and revenue regardless of rating.
⏱️
Non-linear Runtime Effect
Films between 90–140 minutes cluster at the highest average ratings. Very short or very long films show far more variability in audience reception.
💰
Budget Predicts Revenue
Pearson r=0.685 between log-budget and log-revenue. High-budget films have 65% profitability rate vs 39% for low-budget. But ROI varies enormously.
⭐
Star Power → Visibility
Well-known personnel reliably boost vote counts and audience reach, but median ratings are identical (6.30) for high-profile and low-profile groups.
🤖
Ensembles Win
Random Forest, Extra Trees, and Gradient Boosting consistently outperformed linear baselines. Careful data cleaning and log-transforms were essential improvements.
