Insights
Key Findings

What the data revealed about movie success.

🌍
Industry Shapes Ratings
Smaller, more selective industries show higher averages. Larger ones show greater variation — prestige films alongside mass-market productions.
🎭
Genre: Ratings ≠ Votes
Documentary/biopic genres earn high ratings but fewer votes. Action drives audience size without always topping ratings.
⏱️
Non-linear Runtime Effect
90–140 min films cluster at the highest average ratings. Very short or very long films are far more variable.
💰
Budget Predicts Revenue (Imperfectly)
Positive correlation exists, but blockbuster outliers skew distributions heavily. Log-transforming the target stabilises all models.
⭐
Star Power → Visibility
Well-known personnel reliably boost vote counts and audience reach, but do not guarantee high ratings.
📅
Timing Is Not Everything
Release month and season matter, but always in interaction with budget, genre, and cast — not as a standalone driver.
