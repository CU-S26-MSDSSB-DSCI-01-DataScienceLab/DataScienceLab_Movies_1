💡 Key Findings Summary
🌍
Country shapes ratings
Smaller industries rank higher due to curation bias in the dataset.
🎭
Genre: ratings ≠ votes
Documentary = high ratings, low votes. Action = high votes, moderate ratings.
💰
Budget → Revenue (r=0.685)
High-budget films: 65% profitable. Low-budget: 39%. ROI varies enormously.
⭐
Star power ≠ higher ratings
Median rating 6.30 for both high- and low-profile groups. Fame boosts visibility, not quality scores.
🤖
Ensembles win every task
Random Forest and Gradient Boosting outperform linear baselines on all 6 ML questions.
